Make Minecraft as a 3D game that runs in my web browser. It should feel like the real Minecraft, as close as you can get it, and like a finished game, not a prototype.

The complete game:
- Title screen with a menu: Singleplayer (create a new world with a name and a seed, or load a saved one), Options, Quit.
- An infinite, randomly generated block world from the seed: plains, forests, deserts, snowy mountains, oceans and rivers, caves underground with ores (coal, iron, gold, diamond), bedrock at the bottom. Trees, grass, flowers, sand, water.
- First-person view. WASD to move, mouse to look, Space to jump, Shift to sneak, double-tap W or Ctrl to sprint, F5 to switch to third person and see my blocky player.
- Left-click to break blocks (with cracking and a break time that depends on the tool), right-click to place blocks. Dropped items I can pick up.
- Hotbar with 9 slots and the scroll wheel, an inventory screen on E, a crafting table with real recipes (planks, sticks, crafting table, wooden/stone/iron tools, torches, furnace), and a furnace that smelts.
- Survival mode with health, hunger, fall damage and drowning, and Creative mode with flying and every block. Switch in the pause menu.
- Mobs: cows, pigs, sheep and chickens in the day; zombies, skeletons, creepers and spiders at night that attack me and can kill me, then a respawn screen.
- A day and night cycle with a sun, moon, stars and clouds. Torches and lava give off light, caves are dark.
- Water and lava that flow.
- Sounds made in code: footsteps, breaking, placing, mobs, damage.
- Saving and loading worlds in the browser.
- Options: render distance, field of view, mouse sensitivity, volume.
- Make your own pixel-art block textures in the classic 16x16 style. Use original art, no Mojang files.

What you have to work with:
- `bash ../../../tools/shot.sh <out.png> [query] [wait_ms]` takes a 1280x720 screenshot of your index.html running in Chrome. Make index.html support `?autoplay=1` (skip the menus, start a world, and let a computer player walk around, look around, break and place blocks) plus query options to screenshot each menu, night time, a cave and the inventory, so you can see the real game. Look at your screenshots with the Read tool. Use them often, check everything the way a player would see it, and fix anything that looks wrong, empty or basic.
- Libraries load from cdnjs or jsdelivr. Use a recent three.js.
- The game is served over http from this folder.
